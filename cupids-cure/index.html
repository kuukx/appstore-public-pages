Privacy policy

Effective 18 September 2026.

Cupid’s Cure supports relationship journeys, reflections, conversations and optional audio or sketch attachments. Guest journeys are stored on the device in encrypted storage. If you sign in and choose to sync, the shared journey content you select is encrypted before it is synchronized; private entries remain device-only.

The app uses optional microphone and notification permissions only for audio reflections and reminders you choose to enable. It does not sell personal information or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

You can manage app permissions in device settings and request help with account or synced data using the contact below.
