Privacy policy

Effective 18 September 2026.

FitPlanner uses an account email to provide sign-in. It stores the profile details, meal and water entries, workout plans and completions, weight records, and preferences you choose to enter. We also process limited technical information needed to secure and operate the service.

FitPlanner does not sell personal information or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. Application data is stored in encrypted AWS services in London, United Kingdom. Database backups are retained for up to seven days.

You can export your data or permanently delete your account from the app’s Account section. Deletion removes application records and the sign-in account; a minimal closure record may remain until issued access tokens expire, and backup copies expire under the backup-retention process.
