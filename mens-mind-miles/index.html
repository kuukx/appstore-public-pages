Privacy policy

Effective 2 October 2026.

Men’s Mind & Miles supports wellbeing through shared journeys, preparation and optional member circles. It is not therapy, medical treatment, or an emergency service.

To provide an account, we process the name, email address and authentication details you provide. When you take part in a journey, we process journey applications, check-ins, circle posts, comments, private reflections, reports, blocks, and preferences that you choose to create. We process device push tokens only if you opt in to notifications, and use your camera only when you choose to scan an organiser’s check-in code.

We process limited service and security information, such as identifiers, timestamps and diagnostic information, to operate, protect and improve the service. Information is not sold, used for cross-app advertising, or shared with data brokers.

Storage, choices and deletion

Account and journey data are processed to provide the member service. You control what you publish to a circle; private reflections remain private unless you choose to share them. You can delete individual reflections, block members, export your account data, or request account deletion from the app. You can also manage optional permissions in device settings.

We process deletion requests after ownership is verified. Until processing is complete, the app may block access to the account. Limited records may be retained for security, fraud-prevention and backup-retention purposes. Data previously copied or saved by another member cannot be recalled. Contact support for help with a data request.

Support and safety

For Men’s Mind & Miles support, privacy requests, export or deletion help, email techloftgh@icloud.com or call +44 7562 814868.

Reports are not monitored as an emergency response channel. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
