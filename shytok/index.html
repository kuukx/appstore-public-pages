Privacy policy

Effective 18 September 2026.

ShyTok lets you protect media and use optional social, commerce and device-network features. Media you choose for the private vault, vault encryption material and access settings are kept on your device in encrypted storage. Optional permissions for camera, photos, microphone and notifications are used only when you choose the related feature.

ShyTok does not sell personal information or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. Where an account or online feature is used, we process the account and feature data needed to provide it and limited technical information needed for security and reliability.

You can remove vault content and revoke app permissions through your device settings. You may request help with account data or deletion using the support contact below.
